Who We Are

Welcome to I-Tech Media

I-Tech Media is a media and digital skills training center in Phnom Penh specializing in IT education and professional certification programs.

We help students, professionals and career changers build practical skills in computer office, graphic design, video editing, 3D, animation and IT — taught hands-on, in a supportive environment and at a price that works.

Our Mission
